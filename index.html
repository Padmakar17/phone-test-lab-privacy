Phone Test Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Phone Test Lab is an Android diagnostic application developed by Padmakar Joshi.

1. Information the App Accesses

Phone Test Lab may access device features and information needed to perform diagnostic tests, including:

  • Internet and network connectivity status
  • Wi-Fi availability
  • Bluetooth availability
  • Location/GPS services availability
  • Camera availability
  • Microphone availability
  • Speaker functionality
  • Vibration functionality
  • Display and touchscreen capabilities
  • Accelerometer
  • Gyroscope
  • Magnetometer
  • Light sensor
  • Proximity sensor
  • Battery status and battery level
  • Device storage information
  • Device manufacturer, brand, model and Android/device information

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by Phone Test Lab is used to:

  • Perform diagnostic tests.
  • Determine whether supported device features are available.
  • Display diagnostic results to the user.
  • Display relevant device information within the application.

3. Data Collection and Sharing

Phone Test Lab does not require a user account.

Phone Test Lab does not sell personal or sensitive user data.

Phone Test Lab does not intentionally share diagnostic information, sensor readings, camera data, microphone data, location data, or device information with third parties.

Diagnostic checks are designed to operate locally on the user's device.

4. Camera and Microphone

Camera and microphone access is used when the user performs the corresponding diagnostic test.

Phone Test Lab does not use the camera or microphone for advertising, tracking, or background monitoring.

5. Location

Location/GPS access is used to check the availability or status of location services as part of diagnostic functionality.

Phone Test Lab does not intentionally collect or transmit the user's precise location to the developer.

6. Sensor Information

Sensor readings such as accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, light, and proximity information may be accessed during diagnostic tests.

These readings are used for the requested diagnostic functionality and displayed to the user when applicable.

7. Device Information

Phone Test Lab may display information such as manufacturer, brand, model, device name, hardware information, Android version, battery information, and storage information.

This information is used for device diagnostics.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Phone Test Lab does not require a user account and does not maintain a developer-operated account database containing user information.

Users can remove application data through Android device settings or uninstall the application.

9. Third-Party Services

Phone Test Lab does not intentionally use third-party advertising or analytics services to collect user information.

10. Children's Privacy

Phone Test Lab is a general-purpose device diagnostic application and is not specifically directed toward children.

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Security

Phone Test Lab is designed to process diagnostic information locally on the user's device whenever possible.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change.

The updated policy will be published at this location.
